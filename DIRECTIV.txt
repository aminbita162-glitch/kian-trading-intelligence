SECTION 01 — PRODUCT MISSION, OPERATING MODES AND COMMERCIAL SCOPE
======================================================================

01.1 Mission
Build a secure, auditable, scalable, cost-aware trading and mining intelligence platform combining deterministic execution, validated quantitative intelligence, specialized agents and human-controlled operational authority. Target: highest-level agentic platform in this domain, ready for initial commercial release.

01.2 Initial Product Scope
Cryptocurrency spot-market intelligence and trading; simulation and paper trading; mining profitability simulation; controlled future mining hardware/pool integration (deferred for physical); MacBook desktop application; iPhone-compatible remote dashboard (PWA + optional native considerations); cloud-based trading execution and scheduling; multi-tenant management; financial accounting, profit policies and notifications; independent safety, security, audit and compliance controls; commercial monetization with defined tiers.

Architecture supports future expansion; stocks, forex, derivatives, leverage and other products are not authorized for initial live operation.

01.3 Operating Modes
SIMULATION: Historical or synthetic data without real financial orders.
PAPER: Simulated trading using approved market data or sandbox environments.
LIVE: Real financial execution through explicitly authorized providers after all technical, security, legal and human approval gates pass.
All interfaces, records and audit events must clearly identify the active mode. A mode change must never silently enable real-money trading.

01.4 Scale and Commercial Objective
Design for multi-tenant operation. Target capacity-planning objective: 10,000 users (validated by measured benchmarks, not claimed). Commercial initial version must support the defined payment tiers and be sellable as a controlled product.

SECTION 02 — ARCHITECTURE DECISION REGISTER (AD-001 to AD-040)
======================================================================

All prior AD-001 to AD-033 remain binding. New decisions:

AD-034 — Commercial Monetization and Payment Tiers
Four levels: (1) Free one-week trial (limited features, no real capital, simulation/paper only); (2) Paid Tier 1 — monthly starting at USD 200 (usage-dependent; basic advanced features); (3) Paid Tier 2 — monthly USD 350; (4) Paid Tier 3 — monthly USD 500. Annual options available for each paid tier at discounted rate (exact discount owner-approved). Usage metering (compute, LLM tokens, data, orders) must be accurate, auditable and enforceable. No paid feature may weaken safety invariants. Withdrawal of funds remains separately gated.

AD-035 — Evidence-Backed Production Candidate
All verification harnesses must be separated from runtime image. Durable transactional storage mandatory for authoritative state. Simulated verification is insufficient for production claims.

AD-036 — Signed Risk Authorization Tokens
Risk Kernel issues short-lived, single-use, cryptographically signed authorization tokens bound to exact order parameters. Exchange adapters reject any order lacking valid token. Agents hold no signing key.

AD-037 — Tamper-Evident Decision Ledger
Hash-chained append-only decision and financial ledger with external anchoring capability. Decision dossiers for every trade.

AD-038 — Graduated Autonomy and Independent Kill Plane
Formal trust levels (L0 observe to L5 higher-cap live) with automatic demotion and signed human approval for increases. Separate out-of-band kill plane capable of cancelling orders and blocking new exposure even if main platform is down.

AD-039 — Agent Runtime Mesh
All agent capabilities flow through a tool broker, untrusted-text firewall, cost router and independent deterministic checker. No direct agent access to exchanges, databases or network.

AD-040 — Statistical Honesty and Strategy Validation Gate
Mandatory pre-registration, trial counter, purged/embargoed CV, Probability of Backtest Overfitting, Deflated Sharpe Ratio and signed model cards. No strategy enters paper or live without passing gate.

Decision Integrity: Every decision maps to requirements, tests and evidence. No decision may be silently removed.

SECTION 17 — ELEVATION PHASES E0 THROUGH E10
======================================================================

All development follows the Universal Phase Execution Protocol (preflight, implement only authorized scope, test, commit, push, verify remote, report ending with "NEXT PHASE NOT AUTHORIZED", HARD STOP).

E0 — TRUTH RESET AND GOVERNANCE REPAIR
Scope: Truth drift, version consistency, CHANGELOG reconstruction, directive lineage, governance precedence, status.yaml as single source of truth, ADR templates for open items, repository visibility decision, honest REAL/SIMULATED/PLANNED labeling.
Acceptance: CI consistency checks green; every public claim links to evidence or is labeled planned.

E1 — SECURITY BLOCKER REMOVAL
Scope: AEAD envelope encryption with KMS interface, durable identity (Alembic, Argon2id, TOTP/WebAuthn, token rotation), central policy engine (deny-by-default), adversarial tests. Close F-SEC-02/03 with evidence.
Acceptance: Cross-tenant rejected at API and DB; old weak crypto removed; tamper tests fail closed.

E2 — DURABLE CORE
Scope: PostgreSQL schemas for all authoritative aggregates, versioned migrations with up/down tests, DB-enforced invariants (balanced journals, unique idempotency, append-only, RLS), transactional outbox/inbox, testcontainers integration tests. Remove authoritative in-memory state.
Acceptance: Crash at transaction boundaries leaves consistent state; no duplicate effects on replay.

E3 — STRUCTURE AND VERIFICATION SEPARATION
Scope: Split monolithic routers; move all simulators, fault injectors, readiness generators and test harnesses outside the deployable image; import-linter; strict typing; Decimal-only lint on financial modules; API versioning and contract tests; coverage/mutation/property reporting.
Acceptance: Production image contains no verification code; layer violations fail CI.

E4 — SAFETY PLANE HARDENING
Scope: Signed risk authorization tokens; separate kernel and vault trust domains; policy-as-code; formal models (TLA+/P) for order/session/reservation/SAFE_HALT checked in CI; Hypothesis stateful tests; hard gates.
Acceptance: Tampered/expired/replayed tokens rejected; formal invariants hold.

E5 — SIMULATION AND TRUTH INFRASTRUCTURE
Scope: Deterministic Simulation Universe (DST) with nightly multi-seed runs and replay; microstructure-realistic market twin; adapter conformance suite; snapshot reconciliation; real load (k6/Locust) and chaos drills in ephemeral environments; real PITR restore with ledger diff.
Acceptance: Same seed produces identical results; reality gap tracked; performance evidence recorded.

E6 — AGENT RUNTIME AND EVALUATION
Scope: Agent runtime specification; tool broker (allowlist, schema, rate, budget, logging); untrusted-text firewall and injection corpus; independent numeric checker; cost router with per-tenant budgets and graceful degradation; model/prompt registry; golden suite (≥200 scenarios) and red-team arena; context isolation.
Acceptance: Zero policy violations on suite; no direct agent imports of exchange/DB/network; budget exhaustion degrades safely.

E7 — STRATEGY SCIENCE
Scope: Research registry; validation gate with CPCV, PBO, Deflated Sharpe, trial counter (hash-chained); data-quality pipeline; regime/drift monitors; champion/challenger shadow mode with counterfactual attribution; stress scenario library; signed model cards.
Acceptance: Deliberately overfitted strategy is rejected; no strategy enters paper without signed gate report. Explicit statement: passing is not a claim of profitability.

E8 — EVIDENCE, OBSERVABILITY AND SUPPLY CHAIN
