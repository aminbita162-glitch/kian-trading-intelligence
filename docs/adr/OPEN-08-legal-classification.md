# ADR-OPEN-08: Legal Classification and Commercial-Release Eligibility

## Status
PROPOSED — Owner approval required before implementation.

## Context
The platform involves cryptocurrency trading and mining intelligence. Legal classification
is required before any paid commercial feature (AD-034, Section 25). This is a prerequisite
for E10 (Commercial Monetization) and any LIVE trading authorization.

## Decision
[REQUIRES OWNER DECISION — OD-9: Engage legal counsel for commercial classification
before any paid live feature.]

Items to determine:
- Jurisdictional classification of the platform (tool vs. service vs. financial product)
- Applicable regulatory framework (securities, commodities, money transmission, none)
- Required licenses, registrations, or exemptions
- Consumer protection and disclosure requirements
- Data handling and privacy obligations (GDPR, CCPA, others)

## Consequences
- Legal classification determines whether paid features are permissible.
- May require licenses, registrations, or structural changes before commercial launch.
- Non-compliance carries legal and financial risk.

## Alternatives Considered
- Proceed without legal review (NOT acceptable — Section 00.4 prohibits)
- Restrict to simulation/paper only indefinitely (reduces commercial viability)

## Compliance
Interacts with: Section 00.4 (Absolute Prohibitions — no production spending without
counsel authorization), AD-029 (Jurisdiction-aware compliance), E9 (Compliance Package),
E10 (Commercial Monetization).
